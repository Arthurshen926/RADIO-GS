\begin{abstract}
A stronger teacher match does not necessarily yield a better complete answer
to a scene query. An alternative may repair identity while losing object extent,
or replace an already correct response with a plausible distractor. We study
one decision: when is replacing an existing complete response beneficial?
Frozen providers supply an explicit incumbent and intact alternatives. A
teacher-order-invariant signature measures observable query--response agreement,
and a set-conditioned utility predictor learns actual relative answer quality from
separate labelled scenes. One shared calibration rule chooses whether to retain
the incumbent or return a whole alternative. No teacher representation, feature
field or complete membership decoder is retrained. We implement joint text and
real-image supervision with original image teachers and held-out reference
observations, together with query-blind and correspondence-permutation controls.
An exact candidate-pool decomposition separates available answer quality from
decision regret. Current measured transfer covers text and image instance
queries and complete category menus, with actual point responses preserved.
The shared policy preserves all three ScanNet menu metrics and improves UQIS
image set IoU by 0.97 pp, while text remains unchanged. Gains over query-blind and
permuted controls remain statistically inconclusive on the nine opened rooms.
LERF and NVOS transfer remain unfinished; the full-system reference and historical provider
gains are reported separately. This working manuscript does not claim a
validated universal replacement for the complete retained system.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and intended contribution}
Existing scene-query systems already possess useful but nonidentical abilities:
native teacher matching identifies candidates, geometric observations constrain
their support, and complete member decoders recover extent. Replacing those
abilities can lose performance even when the underlying features are retained.
Adding observations or optimizing their reconstruction more accurately can also
damage a correct answer. Our research variable is the \emph{decision over
preserved complete answers}.

We formulate the intervention as relative task utility, rather than teacher
confidence, anchor correctness or source fitting error. The proposed mechanism
couples complete functional candidates with explicit retention, query-conditioned
observable signatures with a consistent utility comparison, and one
independently calibrated selective replacement rule. These are stages of one
decision mechanism, not three representation networks. The empirical contribution
must establish that query correspondence improves decisions beyond structural
answer priors, and that repairs do not cause greater damage to correct responses.
Neither follows merely from fitting the utility regressor.

\section{Independent joint supervision}
We retain the fixed 22-room fit, three-room calibration and four-room development
split. These room identities do not overlap the benchmarks. The complete member
provider also used the fit rooms; this inherited overlap is explicit. The
development rooms and benchmarks were already opened in earlier research, so
they are not an untouched final test.
Calibration rooms are disjoint from fitting, but their paired-model feedback
motivates the set-utility architecture test. They therefore are not an untouched
model-selection test either; no statistical risk guarantee is claimed.

Text supervision reuses sealed natural-language episodes and all candidate
utilities. At most 32 eligible expressions per fit room are selected by a fixed
hash; all calibration and development expressions are included. The deployed
scope response is the text incumbent. Official instance annotations and original
RGB-D construct natural square image references. Annotated geometric visibility
greedily selects up to four reference views; constructor image quality chooses
each target's crop among those views. This uses neither teacher predictions nor
model utility. An initial fixed-temporal-view preparation was stopped before
model fitting because it missed targets in some rooms; it is not an accuracy
experiment.

Reference frames, temporal neighbors and nearby camera poses are excluded
jointly from every image candidate provider. We keep at least half of original
mapping views, two target-observing views and 50\% target visibility. The official
full-scan mesh remains the carrier; geometry itself is not held out. Original
SAM regions and SigLIP coordinates are retained. Original DINO local symmetric
Chamfer is computed from streamed patches with the same quantization and
regional weights. No target mask is supplied to either image query encoder.
The direct member response is the image incumbent.

The protocol produces 290 image episodes across all 29 rooms. Fit, calibration
and development use 237, 28 and 25 images respectively, alongside 629, 207 and
175 text expressions. Fit weight is equal per room, modality and query. Neither
modality nor benchmark identity enters the estimator. The SAME threshold must
protect all six calibration room--modality cells. The smallest image calibration
cell contains only two queries, limiting this empirical protection. Automatic
references include blur, partial views and border padding; a 12-query visual
and reconstruction check is not a full corpus audit.

The complete joint corpus also contains 116 whole-category-menu episodes on
the same 29 rooms: one full-map episode and three chronological view folds per
room. All original SigLIP2, Mask-Adapter and literal-category SAM3 paths are
retained. Only the 88 fit-room category episodes enter optimization; calibration
and development use the full-map episode of each room. The shared margin
protects 15 room/output cells. Three view folds do not create independent rooms,
and the full-scan mesh remains shared. Six classes are absent from calibration.

\section{Evaluation and functional attribution}
The full reference cohort has 31 jobs: four LERF scenes supporting semantic 2D
and selected-set projection outputs, eight NVOS requests, ten ScanNet rooms and
nine UQIS rooms. LERF selected sets are evaluated by 2D projection, not independent
3D ground-truth IoU. LERF, NVOS and ScanNet use declared source assistance; UQIS
is map-only at query time. The reference is one fixed recipe, not task-specific
winning configurations.

Four previous full-cohort studies test partial permissions, available-domain
normalization, extent agreement and response-stage reconstruction. More accurate
source fitting lowers objectives in all 22 source-assisted jobs but yields mixed
accuracy. Historical text-admission and local-image/provider controls explain
which capabilities to retain; they are not gains of the new learner. Their
equations and negative results remain in the diagnostic supplement and archived
partial-evidence manuscript. Those failed controls are not stacked into the
proposed mechanism.

The cross-task decision audit recomputes legal source agreement for all 22
source jobs using the exact incumbent and retained available-domain alternative.
It seals label-free choices before joining cached intact-response scores;
target images are not newly rendered. LERF uses one decision per query across
frames, and ScanNet uses one whole menu for all three category splits. Authorized
source Brier agreement selects all ten ScanNet alternatives, harming seven.
The LERF IoU gain survives spatial correspondence permutation exactly. These
observations do not support source fitting as a sufficient replacement utility.
Source observations also contributed to candidate construction; they are not
independent validation evidence.

Source candidate headroom is substantially smaller than instance-query headroom.
In particular, selecting a menu only for mIoU19 can lose mIoU10 even with a label
oracle. The independent LERF selected set has no alternative in this pool.
Official menu/union target manifests are now prepared on the 29 separate rooms,
with exact official vertex alignment and all 19 classes retained. Three
chronological blocks define 87 cross-view episodes but still only 29 rooms;
boundary pose dependence and full-scan geometry remain. Six classes are absent
from calibration. Original Mask-Adapter responses are now compiled for all
2,643 source views and 97,532 regions, retaining only 19-column cosine responses.
Literal-category SAM3 and foreground/context source evidence are now complete
for all 29 rooms. One joint estimator fits text, image and whole-menu utility,
with one margin protecting five outputs in each calibration room. On the 22 fit
rooms, the finite complete-menu oracle adds only 0.78 pp to the incumbent's mean
menu utility; this is training-set capacity, not benchmark performance. The
registered mesh training carrier differs from the benchmark Gaussian renderer.

Benchmark menu preparation now covers all ten original Gaussian scenes, with
1,158 views and 73,905 regions. Replaying original native and fused responses
gives maximum probability error $7.16\times10^{-7}$ and no changed labels for
any 19/15/10 submenu. The incumbent bytes are retained beside complete
dual-native and source-readout alternatives. These label-free compatibility
checks establish provider preservation, not learned gains. The fixed-policy
category/instance transfer follows complete corpus construction and fitting. Its
Gaussian signature uses positive native-transfer support and per-view region
unions; this narrower domain is not equivalent to full mesh visibility in
supervision. All category and UQIS predictions must precede fresh target scoring.

The full policy improves one image query and harms another; all text, category
and point decisions retain their incumbent. Image IoU exceeds the query-blind
control by 0.61 pp and the permutation control by 0.36 pp. Descriptive scene
bootstrap intervals include zero for both differences. The image result is also
below the earlier instance-only model's 40.99\% IoU. These results do not yet
validate the central query-dependent contribution.

The exact-pool diagnosis leaves 15.39 pp available text IoU gain entirely unused,
and 8.52 pp image decision regret against 9.49 pp available gain. Complete-menu
mean-utility capacity is 0.91 pp (0.85 pp when protecting all three outputs).
Thus the instance decision and the category candidate family have different
limitations; adding teachers or reducing fitting loss alone cannot be taken as
resolving either. The NVOS cache has one target region per view, so the current
within-view comparison signature supplies no verification evidence there.

Full closure requires effective source-assisted observation signatures and a
better verified decision over useful candidates, followed by a fixed-policy five-output
evaluation. Retained scores for unfinished adapters cannot count as new mechanism
successes. An untouched test and broader calibration coverage are also needed.
The scientific default remains v10 until the joint gate is met.

\section{Reproducibility}
The registry binds configuration, source snapshots, input hashes, model seals,
candidate hashes and complete metrics. Only compact candidate signatures,
utility targets, construction coordinates and the small fitted model are
retained. Teachers, raw scans and maps are referenced in place; dense predictions
and image patch features are transient. A small qualitative reference figure is
retained separately. Storage and compatibility checks support the experiment and
are not claimed as algorithmic contributions.
