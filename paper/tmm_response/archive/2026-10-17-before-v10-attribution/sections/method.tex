\section{Query-conditioned partial-evidence fusion}
\label{sec:method}
The method question is whether an observation is entitled to change the
response to a particular query. We organize this decision as one mechanism:
\emph{query applicability, spatial evidence authority, and geometric fusion}.
These are successive decisions about the same evidence, not three independent
networks. The strongest existing implementation supplies concrete instances of
this mechanism; a single admission rule with demonstrated gains across every
input type is still under development.

\paragraph{Frozen providers and legal evidence.}
For query $q$, a frozen provider supplies an initial response $r_0$, legal
observations $\mathcal E_q$, and any independently produced set $S_0$.
An observation supplies a target $y_e\in[0,1]$, a nonnegative geometric
operator $A_e$, and visible support $v_e$. Nonempty rows of $A_e$ are
normalized; invisible rows do not contribute. Native text similarities,
local image correspondence and explicit point support remain in their
respective matching spaces. Their feature vectors need not share a dimension.
The mechanism consumes responses and evidence, not an average of raw teacher
vectors. Inputs obey the original access ledger: source-assisted observations
for LERF/NVOS/ScanNet, map-only observations for UQIS at query time.

\paragraph{Query applicability: which observations may participate?}
Let $g_e(q)$ denote evidence that observation $e$ refers to the requested target
or category union. Geometry alone cannot supply this judgment. The existing
text implementation compares native scores \emph{within the candidates
already detected for the same query}. It compares one- and two-component
score models rather than rejecting candidates against a scene-wide percentile.
Writing their Bayesian information criteria as $B_1,B_2$, the implemented
candidate support is
\begin{equation}
 a_e=(1-\pi_2)+\pi_2\gamma_{\mathrm{high}}(s_e),
 \qquad \pi_2=\sigma((B_1-B_2)/2),\qquad
 g_e=\mathbf 1[a_e\geq 0.5],
 \label{eq:admission}
\end{equation}
where $\gamma_{\mathrm{high}}$ is responsibility for the higher-score component.
The one-component hypothesis supplies no relative identity veto. Insufficient
samples, nearly constant scores or unavailable correspondences do not invent
one. Rejected regions are recorded as unknown, not relabelled as background.
These mixture quantities are model support, not calibrated probabilities of
semantic correctness; view or crop variation can also explain score modes.
Equation~\ref{eq:admission} has a positive controlled result for text candidates.
It has not been validated as the common rule for reference images, categories
and clicks. Those paths currently retain their effective identity providers.

\paragraph{Spatial authority: what may an admitted observation assert?}
Admission of an observation does not establish complete object extent. We
represent positive and exclusion domains by disjoint masks $p_e,n_e$ and their
reliabilities by $c_e^+,c_e^-$. The admitted weight is
\begin{equation}
 w_e(q)=v_e g_e(q)\big(c_e^+p_e+c_e^-n_e\big).
 \label{eq:authority}
\end{equation}
This notation describes an evidence contract; it does not establish that the
estimated masks or weights are correct. No detection, occlusion and identity
rejection do not by themselves license exclusion. Conversely, an explicit
but fallible reference-mask estimate or reliable rival-category evidence can
supply exclusion under its declared assumptions. The retained LERF path
also vetoes negative observations where native positive support exists;
NVOS uses tracked negative reliability; ScanNet preserves its actual source
weights and both native priors. These inherited rules are disclosed, not
claimed to be the same learned authority estimator.

The contract must also distinguish an observed part from a complete member
estimate. In UQIS, the retained image path selects an estimated regional anchor
and reuses the actual-point membership classifier. Its implementation forces
even this estimated anchor to probability one. Thus its declared identity
uncertainty does not yet enforce different positive authority: distinguishing
estimated anchors from explicit clicks remains an implementation gap, not an
achieved property of our method. All nine retained maps use this direct-anchor
provider, not the optional query-conditioned membership candidate. Complete
extent uses map observations and inherited instance supervision. A region merely
containing an anchor also does not justify raising every element in that region;
the post-refinement controls measured this separate failure.

A targeted control now removes only the estimated-anchor override, preserving
the selected identity, classifier and threshold. Its raw conditional member
score at every image anchor remains above threshold, so all actual sets are
unchanged. Conditional membership $m(i\mid h,\mathcal E)$ is not evidence that
identity hypothesis $h$ answers query $q$: it cannot substitute for applicability
$g(h,q)$. Consequently, changing a single positive assignment does not repair the
whole response conditioned on an incorrect identity. This distinction sharpens
the admission problem without adding another module or retraining the provider.

\paragraph{Geometric fusion and unsupported responses.}
After admission, define evidence mass and response sum
\begin{equation}
 m=\sum_e A_e^\top w_e,\qquad
 b=\sum_e A_e^\top(w_e y_e).
\end{equation}
The retained simple fusion path computes
\begin{equation}
 r_i=\begin{cases}
 (b_i+\lambda_i\bar r_i)/(m_i+\lambda_i),&m_i>\epsilon,\\
 r_{0,i},&m_i\leq\epsilon,
 \end{cases}\qquad \epsilon=10^{-8}.
 \label{eq:consensus}
\end{equation}
LERF uses $\lambda=0$; ScanNet retains its original two-native mean
$\bar r$ and $\lambda=0.1$ times visible mass. Unlike a positive-only residual
rule, consensus need not monotonically increase a response whenever an
observation is marked positive. The evidence contract and the numerical update
must not be conflated. NVOS retains its original robust rendered solver, with
a neutral initial response. Section~\ref{sec:stage-study} compares solvers on
the same admitted observations; a common inverse solver is not required or
supported by those results. Unsupported-value preservation applies inside the
core; the retained output adapter may still abstain or zero unobserved elements.

Continuous response $r$ and independent set $S_0$ enter the fixed task readout.
Semantic thresholding, category competition and actual instance selection
retain their distinct definitions. A frozen set does not become a newly
improved output merely because the continuous response changes. UQIS complete
membership is currently a preserved provider, not an implementation of
Equation~\ref{eq:consensus}.

\paragraph{What is settled, and what is still a hypothesis?}
The scope, evidence contract, provider boundary and intervention position are
settled for this working method. The text admission and admitted-response-use
controls provide positive local evidence. Extracted reconstruction cores
preserve the original functional behavior. The unresolved component is a
concrete transferable rule for $g_e,p_e,n_e,c_e^+,c_e^-$ that improves strong
providers across query types. A shared interface alone does not resolve it.
Active-residual normalization, scope agreement and replacement inverse solvers
are diagnostic alternatives, not additional modules in the proposed method.
