\section{Discussion and limitations}
The current evidence supports preserving complete functional responses and
measuring candidate capacity separately from selection quality. It does not
yet show that the learned decision consistently exploits query correspondence.
Its strongest current gain is confined to one image query; the matched null
constraint control is identical. This limits the empirical claim even though
the method has a common mathematical and software interface.

The next substantive experiment must address the failure sites identified by
the controls. Instance transfer needs a better ranking signal over useful
intact candidates. Category transfer has a smaller measured candidate ceiling.
NVOS has only one retained target region per view, so within-view competition
alone supplies no verification signal. LERF requires preserving the distinction
between semantic response and independent selected-set projection. These
interface limitations must be resolved and then evaluated with the same
complete five-task protocol; increasingly conservative retention is not a
substitute for effective transfer.
The NVOS protocol audit further separates weak complete-target responses from
thin-structure/background confusion. A GT-selected 99-threshold grid reaches
90.0921\% mean IoU, but is strictly diagnostic and cannot be promoted. A single
borrowed threshold harms trex, so this evidence does not justify per-scene
threshold tuning or attribute the full external gap to RGB access.

The current fit/calibration/development rooms are disjoint from the benchmarks,
but calibration and development feedback and repeated benchmark inspection have
informed model design. New untouched rooms, broader calibration class coverage,
matched cross-teacher tests and full query latency measurements remain necessary.
Reusing published method comparisons is appropriate for context, while causal
claims depend on the matched-input mechanism ablations. The supplied-mesh and
source-assisted tracks must not be represented as RGB-only or map-only results.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
One registry defines publication sources, metric names, cohorts and the citation
correction ledger. Builders verify raw reproduction hashes and recompute local
aggregates before generating tables. Code/configuration, model seals, candidate
hashes, per-query metrics and compact reports persist; teachers and maps are
referenced in place. Dense arrays are transient. The complete chronological
research record and negative results remain archived separately from the main
argument. Publication-table generation does not run training or inference.

\section{Conclusion}
We formulate query-response refinement as selective replacement of an intact
answer, with frozen providers, query-conditioned utility and one shared
retention rule. Five-task comparisons and matched controls provide a coherent
evaluation framework. The measured results preserve established capability but
do not yet close the intended cross-task contribution. The finite-pool
decomposition identifies ranking error and candidate limitations as concrete
targets for completing that contribution.
