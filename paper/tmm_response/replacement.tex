\paragraph{Evaluation and attribution.}
Use 22 independent fit rooms, three calibration rooms and four previously opened
development rooms. The frozen member provider also used the fit rooms; disclose
that overlap. Train on at most 32 deterministically selected eligible natural
expressions per fit room, then evaluate all calibration/development expressions.
The joint extension also constructs real RGB reference crops from those same
independent rooms, using official instance annotations only for training-data
construction and complete-answer utility targets. Greedy geometric visibility
coverage proposes up to four reference views. Reference frames, their temporal neighbors, and nearby
camera poses are withheld jointly from every candidate provider. Original
SigLIP descriptors and SAM masks are retained; original DINO local matching is
streamed without storing another dense feature map. The full-scan mesh remains
the geometric carrier. This is not a reconstruction experiment with held-out
geometry. Text starts from the deployed scope incumbent and images from the
deployed direct-member incumbent. Only a single shared decision model is fitted.
Seal the model before development and benchmark scoring. Test the same policy
on text and image queries, including one- and two-teacher inputs, without new
benchmark margins. Compare against the same learner without query features and
against a joint within-view permutation of all teacher scores. The latter keeps
their correlations and multiset, geometry and candidate pool unchanged.

The current adapter operates on regional instance evidence. LERF semantic and
projected-set outputs, NVOS reference responses, and ScanNet's complete category
competition require their own legal observation adapters and validation under
the same decision rule. They must not be called successful transfers merely
because their retained reference outputs are unchanged. The finite-pool
selection layer is an active candidate, not yet a validated replacement for
the full v10 system. The accompanying experiment report supplies measured
results; historical text-admission gains belong to our established method and
are not attributed to this learner.
