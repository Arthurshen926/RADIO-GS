## Why a structural change, not a threshold sweep
Independent outer inspection of corrected-duplicate Stage1 on figurines found 62 admitted positive region/query pairs and 865 negative candidate pairs, but 100% of admitted positive head outputs >1.9 (maximum2), 0 negative coefficients enabled, only0.19517% of element/query pairs changed, 0 positive pairs on source-untagged regions. B1 and B2 source held-view Brier differ around1e-8. These diagnostics use saved parent queries/responses, not GT tuning. They falsify a claim that the intervention currently supplies useful identity discrimination. A source numerical passed flag is not substantive evidence.

Root causes to address together:
1. Source-text anchors enter both hard routing and head features; query identity is pre-decided by old source labels. New visual information cannot admit unlabelled regions. Held phrase labels also remain in deployed bank anchors, so head holdout is not an independent visual identity test.
2. A shared signed scalar is clipped positive on P and negative on N. When driven globally positive, its N branch has no gradient. Source renderer objective can be minimized by constant positive saturation without learning appearance-dependent membership.
3. Evidence that a crop contains q is not evidence that the whole scope belongs to q. Need supervision for degree of region membership and its known coverage, not mere label presence.

### Region-membership supervision from independent source views
Reuse existing source P/N/U episodes and fixed renderer. For each episode v,q and region k from a DIFFERENT, non-held prototype view, compute the scope projection A_vk=R_v M_k. Use actual geometry contribution, not equal region votes. Derive p_mass=sum A*P, n_mass=sum A*N, u_mass=sum A*U, known_fraction=(p_mass+n_mass)/(p_mass+n_mass+u_mass), soft membership y=p_mass/(p_mass+n_mass). Fit only region-query pairs with enough visible projected support and known_fraction>=0.5, weighted by known fraction; minimum effective area fixed20 pixels after appropriate per-column support normalization. Report coverage and discarded/ambiguous counts. This is known-domain pseudo membership with coverage filtering, not verified complete object truth. A containing parent whose only known part is a tiny positive child should be UNKNOWN rather than taught y=1. Child contained in a legitimate whole-query positive may have y=1. No explicit physical part/instance labels inferred from containment.

Use direct per-query/view, per-class balanced region membership risk as principal training signal, plus the existing actual deployed element-logit->sigmoid->source renderer partial-known risk (weight0.25) and visible parent-deviation regularizer(0.1). Region risk prevents the signed transport dead-gradient shortcut. If labels are predominantly unknown or one class missing, report the support rather than manufacturing negatives. Ensure balancing is query-view first, then classes present, then average; do not weight a mixed/one-class episode incorrectly. Region classification confidence is distinct from geometric support precision.

### Conditional transport, not a free residual amplifier
After the visual head yields r_k(q), establish positive support with r>=0.8. Negative correction requires r<=0.2 AND a high-confidence positive, geometrically disjoint co-view competitor (retain explicit overlap abstention). Everything else remains unknown; labels and text anchors never enter routing. These fixed posterior confidence thresholds are declared before new target scoring, not tuned per scene. A low visual score alone is not a negative label.

Compare the calibrated membership logit to the weighted parent probability inside the same scope: d_k=clip(logit(r_k)-logit(parent_region_probability),-2,2). Positive-qualified supports only transmit positive d; negative-qualified supports only transmit negative d. Confidence c_k=2*abs(r_k-0.5), optionally existing quality only (fixed, no new learned amplitude). Element logit z_i=b_i+sum M_ik*c_k*d_k, with corrected duplicate/view normalization. No support => exact parent. Parent remains a prior, not identity-head input/label. Confidence gates may be nondifferentiable, but direct visual membership loss must keep both classes' gradients alive. No double odds; use original finite parent logits for all nonzero updates, and preserve exact zero fallback. Both r and actual corrections must vary across queries; record positive and negative active fractions, saturation and untagged-region admissions, not just maximum change.
