# RADIO-GS manuscripts and current research claims

**Active paper (2026-10-16):** [TMM query-response refinement](tmm_response/README.md).
One complete-response retain/replace mechanism over frozen providers; method working
draft in `tmm_response/main.tex`, source-verified tables in `tmm_response/tables/`.
The retained complete v10 system is the performance reference. Four full-cohort
controlled studies are complete; shared cross-task gains remain unproven. The
study registry and unified entry point are documented in the active paper guide.
Feature dimension/compression and
compact-field redesign are outside this paper's research variables.

The active concrete algorithm is now learned complete-response replacement over
both frozen instance providers, described in [replacement.tex](tmm_response/replacement.tex).
The [L01/L02 report](../docs/experiments/2026-10-16-tmm-learned-response-replacement.md)
separates independent development and text/image transfer. The earlier partial-
evidence controls remain background evidence; their gains do not belong to the
new learner. Cross-task transfer and image preservation remain unresolved.

The main manuscript now centers the learned complete-answer decision. The old
partial-evidence manuscript is preserved at
`tmm_response/archive/partial_evidence_main_20261016.tex`. D01/L03--L06 cover real
image supervision, joint pairwise learning and its controlled set-utility
architecture variant. Their reports keep negative controls and unfinished
whole-cohort transfer explicit; none is promoted by documentation alone.

The earlier broad system implementation is documented in
[SYSTEM_IMPLEMENTATION.md](../docs/SYSTEM_IMPLEMENTATION.md), with scientific
acceptance criteria in [CVPR_RESEARCH_PLAN.md](CVPR_RESEARCH_PLAN.md).
The current source-assisted native field is not the compact RADIO reconstruction
model described by the June manuscripts. Their architecture, storage tables and
results must not be reused as claims for the new system. A submission-ready CVPR
manuscript has not yet been established.
